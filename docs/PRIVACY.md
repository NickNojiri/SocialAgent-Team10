# SpotBot privacy policy

SpotBot is a student project (CSULB capstone) that turns social-media posts people share with
it into suggestions of places to go with their friends. Last updated 2026-10-24.

## What we collect

- **Posts you send SpotBot.** When you share a reel or post with SpotBot's Instagram account,
  or paste a link in a Discord server that uses SpotBot, we receive the link, the post's caption
  and, where available, its video. We read the public post page for details such as the venue
  name and the location tag.
- **Who sent it, in a reduced form.** We keep a one-way hash of your Instagram account id, not
  the id itself, to limit how many posts one person can send per hour. We use your id itself
  only to reply to your message.
- **Votes and plans** in Discord servers that use SpotBot, tied to that server.

We don't collect passwords, contact lists or anything from your account beyond the messages
you send SpotBot.

## How we use it

Only to build the "spot cards" (place, area, what it's about) that you and your group see and
vote on, and to measure and improve how accurately SpotBot reads posts. The language models and
the database run on our own computer; we don't sell data or send post text to other companies.
We don't show ads.

## Keeping and deleting it

Saved spots stay until you or your server's admin removes them. A server admin can delete all
of a server's data with SpotBot's delete command. To have anything you sent deleted, message
SpotBot's Instagram account or email the address below, and we'll remove it within 30 days.
Temporary copies of shared posts are deleted automatically after 7 days.
