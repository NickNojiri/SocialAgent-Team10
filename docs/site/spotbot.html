How it works

Three steps from "we should go" to going.

1
📲

Share a reel

Paste an Instagram or TikTok link in your server, or DM it to SpotBot on Instagram.

2
🧠

SpotBot reads it

It listens to what's said, reads the caption and the text on screen, and works out which place it's about.

3
🗳️

Vote, then plan

Everyone taps 👍 on the spots they want. /plan picks one for the group, and a tie gets settled by the picker.

Demo

Friday night, settled in a minute.

Two friends share two reels. The server votes. It's a tie, so SpotBot spins for it.

  • Cards look the way the bot really draws them: the place, a short description, a map link and the buttons.
  • Votes are per person, so one friend can't stuff the ballot.
  • Ties get settled by a random pick, out in the open. (The tie-breaker is the next feature we're building; this is how it will look.)
# weekend-plans · demo
Not every reel is a place

Skits and memes don't break it.

Not every reel is a restaurant. When it isn't, SpotBot still says what it's about in a plain card and files it under 💭, out of the way of the spots you'll actually vote on.

By the numbers

Measured, not guessed.

We hand-labeled hundreds of real reels and test against a split the model never trains on.

74%Right place, 3 in 4 reelsOur trained model on 271 held-out dev reels. Hand-written rules alone: 62%.
~0.3sPer reelOn a laptop CPU, no LLM needed for the place name.
0Posts sent to outside AI servicesThe language models and the database run on our own machine.
1,196Automated testsRun on every change, including security checks on who can read and vote.
Privacy

Your reels stay yours.

🖥️

Runs locally

Post text is read by models on our own computer, never sent to an outside AI company.

🚫

No ads, no selling

SpotBot only sees what you send it, and doesn't sell or share it.

🗑️

Delete anytime

A server admin can wipe the server's data with one command.
